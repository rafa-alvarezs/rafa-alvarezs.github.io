Hi! I am Rafa, a Machine Learning and Data Science undergraduate student @ UofT with a strong interest in research-driven problem solving.

What draws me most to research is the process itself: asking the right questions, designing careful approaches, and using evidence to arrive at conclusions that actually hold up. I’m especially passionate about research at the intersection of **statistics and machine learning**, where theory, data, and real-world uncertainty come together. I enjoy thinking deeply about how models are built, what assumptions they rely on, and how results should be interpreted rather than taken at face value.

I naturally try to ground decisions in as much evidence as possible. Whether I’m working on a technical project or making design choices, I tend to ask *what the data says*, *what assumptions are being made*, and *how confident we can really be*. 

Some areas I’m especially interested in exploring further:
- Research in statistics and machine learning  
- Applied data analysis and model evaluation  
- Evidence-based decision making and automation  
- Healthcare and biomedical applications of data science  

This site is a collection of projects and ideas that reflect how I think about data, research, and problem solving — and how I’m continuing to grow in these areas.
